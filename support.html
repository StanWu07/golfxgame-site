GolfXGame Support / 技术支持

For help with GolfXGame, email worldwildwu@outlook.com.

Include your app version, iPhone/iPad model, a description of the problem and, if helpful, a screenshot with private information hidden. Never send your Apple password, verification codes, wallet seed phrase or private keys.

Joining a match

All players should use the same current version. Open Join a Match and scan the host's QR code or enter the six-character room code. Invitation links require GolfXGame to be installed. The host starts once all seats are filled. Before play begins, guests can leave; in four-ball, they can switch to an available seat on the other team.

Virtual points

New accounts receive 1,000 free virtual points. Points have no cash value and cannot be purchased, withdrawn or exchanged for cryptocurrency. Competition ratings are separate from entry points.

Connection or scoring issues

Refresh the match when connectivity returns. If the app offers to retry your previous action, use that recovery action instead of creating another match. Every participant confirms the final result before settlement. Contact support if the saved result does not match the round you played.
