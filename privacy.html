Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026 · 简体中文

This policy describes how GolfXGame handles information when you use its golf match, scoring and leaderboard features. Contact the GolfXGame team at worldwildwu@outlook.com with privacy questions or requests.

Information we process

For online play, Sign in with Apple provides an account identifier and may provide your name when you first authorize the app. We use Supabase to verify your identity and maintain your account and login session. GolfXGame requests your name, not access to your Apple password or email inbox.

We store your nickname, account identifier, nickname-change date, virtual-point balance, ratings, match participation, player-entered course and match names, hole scores, outcomes, result confirmations and point/request records. These support authentication, shared matches, accurate scoring and points, recovery from interrupted requests, and leaderboards.

What other players can see

Your nickname, ratings and leaderboard statistics are visible to other users. Participants can see shared match details and scores. A person with a valid invitation or room code can preview the open room. Avoid including private information in nicknames, course names or match names.

Camera and device storage

Camera permission is used to scan invitation QR codes. Scanning is processed on your device; camera images are not uploaded to the match service. The current app does not request GPS location. A course name you enter may identify where a match takes place. Offline demo players and matches stay on your device and are not added to online leaderboards.

The app stores login credentials in the device Keychain and stores preferences, demo data and pending requests locally.

Service providers

Apple provides sign-in and app distribution. Supabase provides authentication and database hosting; the current cloud project is hosted in the United States. Providers may process network information such as IP addresses and operational/security logs to deliver and protect the service. Support email and information you choose to send are processed through our Outlook email service. Older CloudKit test data remains separate from the current Supabase service.

We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking. The current release does not offer cryptocurrency payments, paid points or cash withdrawals. We may disclose information where necessary to comply with applicable law or protect the service and its users.

Retention and deletion requests

Account and match records are retained to provide account history, shared results and leaderboard functionality. Information may also be retained as necessary for security, dispute resolution or legal obligations; this policy does not promise a fixed automatic deletion period.

To request access, correction or deletion, email worldwildwu@outlook.com. We may need to verify that the request concerns your account. Do not send passwords or verification codes. In-app deletion is not yet enabled in the current build. Shared results involving other players, provider backups and older CloudKit test data require separate consideration; we will explain applicable handling when responding to a request.

Security and updates

We use authenticated access and service access controls to protect information, but no system can guarantee absolute security. This policy may be updated as features or data handling change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 7 日

本政策说明 GolfXGame 如何处理高尔夫比赛、计分和排行榜功能涉及的信息。隐私问题及请求请联系 worldwildwu@outlook.com。

收集的信息与用途

线上模式使用 Apple 登录，接收账号标识，并可能在首次授权时接收姓名。Supabase 验证身份并维护账号及登录会话。App 请求姓名，不读取 Apple 密码或邮箱收件箱。

服务保存昵称、账号标识、昵称修改时间、虚拟积分余额、段位分、参赛记录、用户填写的球场和比赛名称、逐洞成绩、赛果、确认信息及积分和操作请求记录，用于登录、共享球局、计分、积分核对、断网恢复和排行榜。

信息展示与设备权限

其他用户可看到昵称、段位及排行榜统计；参赛者可看到共享球局和成绩。持有效邀请或房间码的人可预览开放房间。请勿在昵称或比赛名称中填写私人信息。

相机仅用于设备端扫描邀请二维码，不向比赛服务上传相机画面。当前 App 不请求 GPS 定位；手动填写的球场名称可能表明比赛地点。本机演示数据仅保存在设备，不进入线上排行榜。登录凭据保存在设备钥匙串，偏好、演示数据和待恢复请求保存在本机。

服务商与信息共享

Apple 提供登录和分发，Supabase 提供身份验证和数据库，当前云端项目位于美国。服务商可能处理 IP 地址及运行、安全日志。用户主动发送的支持邮件通过 Outlook 邮件服务处理。早期 CloudKit 测试数据与当前服务独立保留。

我们不出售个人信息，不将其用于跨 App 广告追踪。当前正式版本不提供虚拟币支付、积分购买或现金提现。必要时可能依法或为保护服务和用户而披露信息。

保留、访问、更正与删除

账号和比赛数据用于维护历史、共享赛果及排行榜；安全、争议处理或法律义务也可能需要保留部分信息。本政策不承诺固定期限的自动删除。

如需访问、更正或删除，请联系 worldwildwu@outlook.com，我们可能需要核实账号归属，请勿发送密码或验证码。当前构建尚未启用 App 内删除。涉及其他球友的共享赛果、服务商备份和旧 CloudKit 测试数据需要分别处理，我们将在回复请求时说明适用的处理方式。

安全与政策更新

我们使用身份验证及访问控制保护数据，但任何系统都无法保证绝对安全。功能或数据处理方式变化时可能更新政策，以页面生效日期为准。
